A Team Is Only as Strong as the Bottom of Its Lineup

The weakest regular starters had a stronger relationship with group-stage results than the biggest stars.

Across 48 teams and 528 players, I measured each team's lineup floor using the average value of the three least-valued players within its most-used group-stage XI.

The idea

Total value can hide a thin lineup floor.

The analysis tested how lineup floor related to group-stage points, group-stage goal difference, and advancement to the knockout stage.

Core XI

Soccer teams field 11 players. Here, Core XI means a retrospective, usage-based XI: the 11 players with the largest actual group-stage roles, prioritized by minutes, then starts, then appearances.

Lineup floor

Lineup floor means the average market value of the three least-valued players within that Core XI. It is not the three cheapest players in the full squad or the cheapest players who appeared briefly.

Why it matters

Total value can be inflated by a few elite players. For non-soccer readers, this is a weakest-recurring-dependency problem.

Strongest evidence

Teams with stronger lineup floors advanced far more often.

Eleven of the 12 teams in the strongest quartile advanced, compared with two of 12 in the weakest quartile.

The strongest lineup-floor quartile averaged 6.58 points and a +4.25 goal difference. The weakest quartile averaged 1.25 points and a -5.17 goal difference.

Case study story

From a depth-risk question to a validated metric.

The problem

Traditional team rankings can hide dependence on a few elite players.

The build

Reconstructed 48 usage-based Core XIs from actual group-stage minutes, starts, and appearances; matched 528 players to valuation data; standardized values in USD; and created a bottom-three lineup-floor metric.

The result

Among the measures tested, lineup floor had the strongest observed relationship with group-stage results and advancement to the knockout stage.

Analyzed 528 players across 48 World Cup teams to develop a lineup-floor metric that correlated 0.771 with group-stage points, exceeding total XI value at 0.719 and top-three star value at 0.699.
Methodology note

Player values were assembled from FootballTransfers, FotMob/SciSports, and Transfermarkt. A source-removal check retained 8 of the 10 teams in the lowest-value group, supporting the directional result while leaving some uncertainty around exact low-end ordering.
